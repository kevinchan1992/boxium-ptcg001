Based on the video provided, here is a detailed description of the image carousel/marquee design and its UX pattern:

**Layout and Overall Presentation**
*   **Format:** The component is a single-row, horizontal scrolling list (a marquee).
*   **Placement:** It is positioned centrally in the viewport, occupying the majority of the screen space below the top header area (which contains a profile icon and the PSA logo).
*   **Background:** The carousel sits on a solid, dark background (black or very dark gray), which creates high contrast and makes the brightly colored items within the carousel stand out.

**Image Characteristics (Size, Aspect Ratio, and Display)**
*   **Content:** The items are images of PSA-graded Pokémon trading cards encased in plastic slabs.
*   **Aspect Ratio:** The images have a distinct portrait (vertical) aspect ratio, roughly 1:1.6, matching the physical dimensions of a graded card slab.
*   **Display Method:** The images are displayed **fully uncropped**. The entire object, from the top edge of the grading label to the bottom edge of the plastic case, is completely visible within the frame.
*   **Size:** The images are relatively large, taking up most of the available vertical space in the lower two-thirds of the screen, allowing the details of the cards and the grading labels to be seen.

**Spacing**
*   There is consistent, uniform spacing (margins) between each image. The gap is moderate—wide enough to clearly define each item as a separate entity, but tight enough to maintain the visual connection of a continuous list. 

**Animation Style and UX Pattern**
*   **Animation:** The carousel utilizes a smooth, continuous **auto-scrolling** animation. The items move at a constant, steady speed from right to left across the screen. 
*   **Interaction:** It does not appear to rely on user swiping or dragging (though touch interaction might pause it, this isn't demonstrated). It functions as an "infinite ticker" or marquee.

**Indicators and Controls**
*   **Visible Controls:** There are **zero** visible navigation controls. 
*   There are no left/right arrows.
*   There are no pagination dots at the bottom.
*   There is no visible scrollbar.

**UX Pattern Summary**
The design pattern is an **Infinite Auto-Scrolling Marquee**. Because it lacks user controls or indicators, the UX is designed for passive consumption rather than active browsing. It is meant to showcase a continuous stream of inventory or highlights, drawing the user's eye through motion rather than requiring them to click or swipe to see more content.
